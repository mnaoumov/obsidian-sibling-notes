# Chapter 2

The second chapter, between [Chapter 1](<./Chapter 1.md>) and [Chapter 3](<./Chapter 3.md>). Its position in the bar above is `3 / 4`, because the File explorer shows the subfolder `Appendix` first.

From the meadow the path turns away from the river and climbs a gentle slope through a wood of beech and oak. The ground is soft with old leaves, and the sound of the water fades behind you until all you can hear is the wind high up in the branches.

Halfway up the slope there is a clearing where a tree came down in a storm years ago. Its trunk lies across the path, and walkers have worn a step into the bark where they climb over it. Moss covers the side that faces north, and small mushrooms grow in a line along the top.

At the top of the slope the wood opens onto a ridge. From here you can see the whole valley: the river winding back the way you came, the bridge as a grey line across it, and the roofs of the village beyond. On a clear day the hills on the far side look close enough to touch.

The ridge path is wide and dry, and it follows the line of an old wall built from flat stones without mortar. In places the wall has fallen, and in others it is still as high as your shoulder. Sheep graze on the far side and watch you without interest.

Where the wall ends, a narrow track leads back down towards the water. It is steep and loose underfoot, so take it slowly. The bottom bar below this paragraph goes on to the last chapter, or back to the first one.
